About us

BudgetSaathi is a free educational website that teaches households to manage money better. We explain budgeting in plain English — and in simple Urdu too.

Here you will find how to build a monthly budget, how the 50/30/20 rule works, and practical tips for cutting daily expenses — plus a free calculator and a printable budget sheet.

Note: everything here is for education only. Talk to a qualified advisor before any major financial decision.
