FAQ

Questions, answered

How long does a project take?

Small web apps: 1–2 weeks. Mobile apps: 3–6 weeks. POS / custom software: 4–8 weeks depending on modules. You get a timeline with your fixed quote.

How do we communicate during the project?

Entirely on WhatsApp — text and voice notes, in English or Urdu. You get demo videos at every milestone, so you always see progress.

How do payments work?

50% advance to start, 50% on delivery. We accept Payoneer, Wise and bank transfer. The quote is fixed — it never grows mid-project.

What if I need changes after delivery?

Two revision rounds are included before final delivery, and bug fixes are free for 30 days after launch. New features later are quoted separately.

Do you also offer graphic design?

Yes — logos, posters, banners, social-media posts, thumbnails and infographics, in English and Urdu. Graphic design has been a studio service since 2020, and designs start at $10. See the Design section for samples, and message us on WhatsApp for a fixed quote.

Do you work with clients outside Pakistan?

Yes — the studio is remote-first and works with clients worldwide. Communication is async-friendly across time zones.
